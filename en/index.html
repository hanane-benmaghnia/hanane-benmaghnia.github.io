About me

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher in the PEQUAN team at LIP6, Sorbonne University, where I work on the ANTIPASTI project in collaboration with industrial partner Thales. My research interests are: numerical validation, trustworthy AI, safety-critical embedded systems, code synthesis, numerical accuracy, and distributed acoustic sensing (DAS). I am particularly interested in computer arithmetic, precision tuning, and reliable deployment of neural networks on embedded systems.

I completed my Ph.D. at LAMPS, University of Perpignan Via Domitia, where I worked on Fixed-Point Code Synthesis for Neural Networks under the supervision of Matthieu Martel, Yassamine Seladji, and Xavier Thirioux. I then joined the FRAMA-C team at the CEA-LIST (French Alternative Energies and Atomic Energy Commission) as an R&D engineer.

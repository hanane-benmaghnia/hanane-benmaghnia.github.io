M1/M2 Internship Opportunities

Nine research internship topics for Master’s students (M1/M2) in computer science, artificial intelligence, and numerical computing.

The 9 available topics

1

Towards Reliable Activation Functions: Polynomial Approximations and Low-Precision Arithmetic

Numerical precision · Implementing and comparing activation functions using floating-point (FP), fixed-point (FxP), lookup tables and polynomial approximations.

2

Efficient Polynomial Approximation for Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks

KANs · Studying polynomial approximation methods for Kolmogorov-Arnold networks (KANs).

3

Interdisciplinary Research in Artificial Intelligence on Alaskan Native Archival Collections

Interdisciplinary AI · Applying AI approaches to archival collections in an interdisciplinary research setting.

4

Design and Implementation of an Arithmetic for the “Law of Large Numbers”

Arithmetic · Studying and implementing numerical arithmetic approaches for the law of large numbers.

5

Compilation and Automatic Transformation of Programs via Specialized Numerical Libraries

Compilation · Detecting numerical operations and transforming programs with LLVM/MLIR and specialized numerical libraries.

6

Federated Learning

Machine learning · Investigating methods for distributed training of machine learning models.

7

Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks and Multilayer Perceptrons

AI architectures · Studying and comparing Kolmogorov-Arnold networks (KANs) and multilayer perceptrons (MLPs).

8

Mixed-Format Quantization of Neural Networks: Fixed-Point, Floating-Point and Entropy Analysis

Mixed precision · Exploring mixed numerical formats, entropy analysis and the detection of inactive neurons.

9

Capturing the Output Half-Spaces of a Deep Neural Network Classifier via Zonotopes

AI verification · Forward and backward propagation, intersections, and approximations of classification regions using zonotopes.
